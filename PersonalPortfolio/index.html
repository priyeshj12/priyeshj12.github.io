About me

I am Priyesh Jagroep, a Data Scientist and Business Intelligence specialist from Almere, working in the Amsterdam region. I work with Python, SQL and modern data platforms to turn data into clear insights and dashboards for organisations.

In my work I combine data engineering with data analysis. At the Dutch Authority for the Financial Markets I build scalable solutions in Microsoft Fabric, Spark and Data Lakes and translate complex financial data into clear reports in Power BI and SSRS. My focus is on clear visualisations, reliable figures and reports that decision makers can use right away.

What I do

  • Dashboards and KPI reporting

    Developing clear Power BI dashboards and reports that allow teams to steer directly on KPIs and risks.

  • Data pipelines and data engineering

    Building scalable data pipelines in Microsoft Fabric, Spark and Data Lakes so that data is reliable and reusable across the organisation.

  • Machine learning and forecasting

    Designing models in Python that forecast trends and peak load and help organisations look ahead instead of only looking back.

  • Data storytelling and knowledge sharing

    Explaining analyses in clear language, giving sessions about data and guiding stakeholders through what the numbers actually mean.
